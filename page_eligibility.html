5-Year Integrated Law Programs — BBA LLB & B.Com LLB

Eligibility Criteria

To apply for the 5-year integrated law programs at CFI College of Law, candidates must meet the following eligibility requirements as per Bar Council of India (BCI) norms and University of Calicut regulations.

BBA LLB (Hons) B.Com LLB (Hons)
01

Educational Qualification

The candidate must have successfully passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary Examination) or any equivalent examination from a recognized board or institution.
Students from Commerce, Science, or Arts streams are eligible to apply — no specific stream is mandatory.
Candidates awaiting their 10+2 results may apply provisionally, subject to submission of proof of qualification at the time of admission.
02

Minimum Marks Requirement (As per BCI Norms)

The percentage calculation will be based on the total aggregate marks obtained in the qualifying examination. Category-wise minimum requirements are as follows:
Category Minimum Aggregate Marks Notes
General Category 45% In qualifying examination
OBC Category 42% In qualifying examination
SC / ST Category 40% In qualifying examination
03

Age Limit

The age of the candidate must be in accordance with the rules and regulations prescribed by the Bar Council of India (BCI) at the time of admission.
Any changes or updates issued by the Bar Council of India will be applicable to all candidates.
04

Admission Criteria

Admission is granted based on merit in the qualifying examination and/or performance in the qualifying entrance examination, as applicable.
Candidates may also be required to attend a counseling session or interview as part of the admission process.
05

Document Verification

All admissions are subject to verification of original documents, eligibility confirmation, and compliance with college and university regulations.
10th & 12th mark sheets, TC & conduct certificates, entrance rank card (if applicable), photographs, and category certificates (if applicable).

Eligibility FAQs

Eligibility FAQs – BBA LLB (Hons) & B.Com LLB (Hons)

1. What is the basic eligibility for BBA LLB (Hons) in Kerala?
Candidates must pass 10+2 from a recognized board with required marks as per BBA LLB admission process Kerala and BCI norms.
2. What is the eligibility for B.Com LLB (Hons) admission?
Students who completed 10+2 in any stream can apply under the B.Com LLB admission procedure Kerala, subject to minimum marks criteria.
3. What minimum marks are required for law admission in Kerala?
As per law college admission guidelines Kerala, minimum 45% (General), 42% (OBC), and 40% (SC/ST) is required for law college 5-year course admission Kerala.
4. Can Science students apply for BBA LLB or B.Com LLB?
Yes, students from Commerce, Science, or Arts streams are eligible under the law college admission procedure Kerala.
5. Is there an age limit for the 5-year law course in Kerala?
Yes, age eligibility must follow the rules prescribed by the Bar Council of India for law college 5-year course admission Kerala.
6. Can students awaiting 10+2 results apply?
Yes, provisional applications are allowed under the step by step law college admission Kerala process, subject to result submission at the time of admission.
7. Is entrance exam mandatory for eligibility?
Eligibility requires 10+2 qualification; admission may include merit or entrance evaluation as per law college admission guidelines Kerala.
8. Is Commerce stream compulsory for B.Com LLB (Hons)?
No, students from any stream can apply under the B.Com LLB admission procedure Kerala, if eligibility criteria are met.
9. What documents are required to prove eligibility?
10+2 mark sheet, certificates, ID proof, and category certificates are needed for law college admission procedure Kerala verification.
10. Where can I check complete eligibility details?
Full details are available in the prospectus under how to apply law college Kerala and law college admission procedure Kerala sections.
