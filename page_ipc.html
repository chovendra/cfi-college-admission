Career Opportunities, Internship & Placement Support

For BBA LLB and B.Com LLB Students

The integrated programs of BBA LLB (Hons) and B.Com LLB (Hons) at CFI College of Law, a leading law college in Thrissur Kerala, are designed to give students exactly that edge, ensuring they are job-ready and confident the moment they graduate.

Structured Internship Support

Structured Internship Support

Internships are a core component of our curriculum, giving students real-world experience and an opportunity to apply classroom learning in professional environments:

Judicial & Court Internships

Students work in district courts, high courts, and tribunals, learning courtroom procedures, drafting petitions, and observing litigation firsthand.

Corporate & Law Firm Internships

Partnerships with law firms, corporate legal teams, and compliance departments expose students to corporate law, taxation, banking law, and advisory roles.

NGOs & Social Justice Projects

Internships with NGOs, government agencies, and advocacy groups allow students to contribute to human rights, public interest law, and community legal services.

Research & Policy Internships

Opportunities with think tanks and research organizations strengthen analytical and policy formulation skills.

Faculty guidance ensures students select internships aligned with career goals, while regular mentoring and skill-building sessions enhance employability and professional competence.
Career Opportunities After Graduation

Career Opportunities
After Graduation

Graduates of BBA LLB (Hons) and B.Com LLB (Hons) have access to a wide spectrum of professional opportunities:

Litigation & Advocacy

Become advocates, legal consultants, or associates in law firms and courts.

Corporate Law & Compliance

Work as legal advisors, in-house counsels, compliance officers, or corporate consultants.

Banking & Finance Law

Careers in financial institutions, taxation, auditing, and corporate advisory.

Judiciary & Government Services

Opportunities in judicial services, public administration, regulatory bodies, and policy-making.

Legal Research & Consultancy

Engage in policy analysis, corporate advisory, and strategic legal consulting.

Entrepreneurship & Legal Tech

Start law consultancies, advisory firms, or legal technology ventures.

NGOs & Social Justice

Contribute to public interest law, human rights advocacy, and legal aid initiatives.

Placement & Career Development Support

Placement & Career
Development Support

The college provides a dedicated Placement & Career Development Cell that ensures every student is prepared to step into the professional world with confidence:

1

Industry Tie-Ups & Collaborations

  • Partnerships with law firms, corporate houses, NGOs, and government agencies.
  • Exclusive placement drives, recruitment workshops, and internship tie-ups.
2

Personalized Career Guidance

  • One-on-one mentorship with experienced faculty and career counselors.
  • Guidance on selecting career paths aligned with student interests, skills, and goals.
3

Skill Development & Professional Training

  • Workshops on resume building, interview preparation, professional ethics, legal drafting, and negotiation skills.
  • Soft skills development: communication, leadership, teamwork, and client management.
4

Internship-to-Placement Pathways

  • Top-performing interns often receive pre-placement offers from law firms, corporates, or NGOs.
  • Continuous tracking of internship performance to help students improve and maximize placement opportunities.
5

Alumni Mentorship & Networking

  • Access to alumni in courts, corporate law, banking, government, and consultancy sectors.
  • Networking events, guest lectures, and industry interaction sessions to connect students with potential employers.
6

Placement Success Support

  • Mock interviews, aptitude tests, and case-study sessions to prepare students for competitive job markets.
  • Assistance with documentation, certifications, and professional portfolio creation for smooth onboarding.
Frequently Asked Questions

Internship, Placement &
Career Opportunities FAQs

Q1
What legal internships are available for BBA LLB and B.Com LLB students in a law college in Thrissur Kerala?
Students can intern in courts, corporate law firms, NGOs, and policy research organizations, gaining practical experience in litigation, corporate law, taxation, and human rights.
Q2
Does CFI College of Law provide placement support for BBA LLB and B.Com LLB graduates?
Yes, the Placement & Career Development Cell offers industry tie-ups, career guidance, workshops, and connections with law firms, corporate teams, and NGOs.
Q3
How do internships help in career opportunities after BBA LLB or B.Com LLB?
Internships give real-world experience, improve employability, and often lead to pre-placement offers in law firms, corporates, and NGOs.
Q4
What career guidance is offered for students pursuing corporate law or litigation?
Faculty mentors provide personalized guidance, internship advice, and job preparation for careers in corporate law, litigation, research, and consultancy.
Q5
Can students intern in corporate companies and law firms through the college?
Yes, students get exposure to corporate law, taxation, banking law, and advisory roles through collaborations with top law firms and corporate legal teams.
Q6
Are pre-placement offers (PPOs) available through internships?
Yes, high-performing interns often receive PPOs from law firms, corporates, and NGOs, giving them a head start in their careers.
Q7
What skills do students develop during internships?
Students gain legal drafting, research, communication, negotiation, and management skills, making them ready for careers in litigation, corporate law, or consultancy.
Q8
How does the college prepare students for competitive placements?
Through mock interviews, workshops, case studies, resume building, and portfolio guidance, students are fully prepared for corporate and legal recruitment.
Q9
Are internships mandatory, and how long do they last?
Yes, internships are mandatory. Students complete 4–8 week internships in courts, law firms, corporate offices, and NGOs during the 5-year program.
Q10
How do internship and placement support help in long-term career growth?
Structured internships, mentorship, and placement support help students build practical skills, networks, and confidence for careers in litigation, corporate law, judiciary, and entrepreneurship.
