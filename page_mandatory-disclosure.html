Institutional Information

Name of InstitutionCFI College of Law
LocationPoyya, Thrissur District, Kerala, India
AffiliationUniversity of Calicut
ApprovalApproved by the Bar Council of India (BCI) & Government of Kerala
Type of InstitutionSelf-Financing Law College

CFI College of Law is a professionally managed institution dedicated to delivering quality legal education in accordance with the academic standards and regulatory framework prescribed by the Bar Council of India, the University of Calicut, and the Government of Kerala.

The college operates in full compliance with statutory norms governing infrastructure, faculty qualifications, curriculum delivery, student welfare mechanisms, and institutional transparency. All academic activities are conducted under the guidelines and semester system prescribed by the affiliating university.

The institution is committed to academic excellence, ethical legal practice, and the development of socially responsible legal professionals capable of contributing meaningfully to the justice system and society.

Governing Body / Management Details

CFI College of Law is managed by:

Name of Trust/SocietyCFI Charitable Trust

The CFI Charitable Trust is a registered body established with the objective of promoting quality education and social development initiatives. The Trust oversees the administration, financial management, infrastructure development, policy formulation, and regulatory compliance of the institution.

Role of the Governing Body

The Governing Body of the institution is responsible for:

  • Strategic planning and institutional development
  • Ensuring compliance with BCI, University of Calicut, and Government norms
  • Appointment of qualified faculty and staff
  • Monitoring academic standards and performance
  • Financial supervision and resource allocation
  • Maintaining institutional transparency and accountability

The Governing Body functions in accordance with the Trust's objectives and relevant statutory provisions to ensure that the institution maintains high standards in legal education and professional training.

Vision

Our vision is to build a strong and respected centre for legal education where students feel supported, guided, and inspired throughout their journey. The institution aims to grow as a trusted Law College in India that is known not only for academic quality but also for discipline, transparency, and strong values. We believe that law is not just a subject to study, but a responsibility to understand and practise with honesty. As a developing Law college in Poyya Thrissur Kerala, the goal is to create an environment where students can confidently prepare for their future careers in advocacy, corporate law, judiciary, legal services, and higher studies. The vision is to combine classroom learning with practical exposure so that every graduate leaves with knowledge, confidence, and a clear sense of professional ethics. Step by step, through consistent effort and quality education, the institution aspires to be recognised among the most dependable law colleges for serious legal studies.

4

Mission

Building Strong Legal Professionals Through Meaningful Education

Our mission is centred on students — their growth, their confidence, and their professional success. We aim to create a learning space where students are encouraged to think independently, speak clearly, and understand the deeper purpose of law in society.

  • To provide structured and well-planned academic programs that make legal concepts easy to understand, while also maintaining seriousness and academic discipline.
  • To offer 5 year integrated BBA LLB and B.Com LLB in Kerala, allowing students to study law together with business and commerce subjects. This helps them understand how law works in companies, financial systems, and corporate environments, opening wider career paths.
  • To deliver high-quality BBA LLB (Hons) & B.Com LLB (Hons) courses/education that focus on building practical skills such as drafting legal documents, analysing case laws, participating in moot courts, improving public speaking, and strengthening research ability.
  • To strengthen Integrated Law Courses Kerala by including internships, court visits, seminars, workshops, legal awareness programs, and academic mentoring so students gain real-world exposure along with theoretical knowledge.
  • To encourage active classroom participation where students can ask questions, engage in discussions, take part in debates, and develop confidence in expressing legal opinions.
  • To promote research habits and critical thinking so students learn to examine laws carefully, understand policy decisions, and stay updated with legal developments in India.
  • To teach the importance of honesty, discipline, constitutional values, and social responsibility so that students become ethical professionals who respect justice and fairness.
  • To maintain high academic standards as a BCI (Bar Council of India) approved college in Kerala, ensuring that all programs follow proper guidelines, regulatory requirements, and transparent administrative practices.
  • To provide continuous academic guidance, mentoring support, and personal attention so that students improve not only in marks but also in communication skills, leadership qualities, and professional behaviour.

Through consistent effort, student care, and practical training, the mission is to shape graduates who are confident in the courtroom, responsible in society, and prepared for the challenges of the legal profession.

Academic Programme

CFI College of Law offers integrated undergraduate law programmes approved by the Bar Council of India (BCI) and affiliated to the University of Calicut. The programmes are designed to combine professional legal education with strong foundations in commerce and business administration, preparing students for diverse career pathways in law, corporate sectors, judiciary, and academia.

BBA LL.B. (Hons.) – Five-Year Integrated Programme
  5 Years (10 Semesters)   University of Calicut   BCI Approved

The BBA LL.B. (Hons.) programme integrates Business Administration subjects with core legal studies. This programme is structured to equip students with managerial insight along with legal expertise, making it particularly suitable for careers in corporate law, business compliance, taxation, intellectual property, and entrepreneurship.

The curriculum includes:

Principles of Management
Financial Accounting
Business Economics
Marketing & Human Resource Management
Constitutional Law
Criminal Law
Contract Law
Corporate Law
Labour Law
Administrative Law
Intellectual Property Law
Alternative Dispute Resolution
Drafting, Pleading & Conveyancing
Professional Ethics

Students also undergo compulsory internships, moot court training, legal aid activities, and practical training components as prescribed by the Bar Council of India.

B.Com LL.B. (Hons.) – Five-Year Integrated Programme
  5 Years (10 Semesters)   University of Calicut   BCI Approved

The B.Com LL.B. (Hons.) programme combines Commerce subjects with comprehensive legal education. This integrated structure provides students with in-depth knowledge of financial systems, taxation, accounting, and corporate regulations along with core areas of law.

The curriculum includes:

Financial Accounting
Corporate Accounting
Business Law
Income Tax Law
Banking & Insurance
Corporate Governance
Constitutional Law
Law of Crimes
Law of Torts
Company Law
Environmental Law
International Law
Arbitration & ADR
Moot Court & Internship

This programme is ideal for students aspiring to build careers in corporate litigation, taxation practice, financial compliance, corporate advisory, and judicial services.
