About CFI College of Law – Where Your Legal Journey Begins

Poyya, Thrissur – Kerala

If you’ve ever dreamed of standing in a courtroom, defending justice, or building a career that truly makes a difference — CFI College of Law in Poyya, Thrissur (Kerala) is where your journey can begin.

At CFI, we don’t believe in just teaching law; we believe in shaping confident, capable, and ethical legal professionals. The college is affiliated with the University of Calicut and approved by the Bar Council of India (BCI), which means your degree will be recognized across the country and respected in the legal field.

CFI offers two integrated five-year programs:

  • BBA LLB (Hons.)
  • B.Com LLB (Hons.)

These courses are designed for students who want to combine legal education with knowledge in business or commerce. Whether you want to become a corporate lawyer, judge, legal advisor, or crack civil services, these programs will give you a strong foundation and multiple career options.

But what truly makes learning at CFI different is the real-world exposure you’ll get. From moot courts and internships to legal aid clinics, workshops, and expert guest lectures, every part of your education here is designed to help you apply what you learn and grow with confidence. You won’t just be a student — you’ll be a future lawyer in the making.

Our campus in Poyya offers a peaceful, focused environment for learning, with access to all the facilities you need — a legal research library, modern classrooms, and dedicated faculty who guide you every step of the way.

If you're looking for a student-first law college in Kerala that supports your dreams and prepares you for success, CFI College of Law is the place to begin. Your future in law starts here — and it starts strong.
