Step-by-Step Law College Admission Procedure Kerala

How to Apply at
CFI College of Law

Follow our step-by-step guide to secure your seat and begin your legal career with confidence. The process is transparent, student-friendly, and guided at every stage.

1
Step 1

Check Eligibility

Ensure you meet the eligibility requirements before applying for either program.

  • BBA LLB admission process Kerala: Completion of 10+2 or equivalent with minimum marks as per college guidelines.
  • B.Com LLB admission procedure Kerala: 10+2 completion in any stream is acceptable.
  • Ensure you meet age and academic requirements before applying.
2
Step 2

Obtain Prospectus & Application Form

Download or collect the college prospectus to review law college admission guidelines Kerala, eligibility, fee structure, and course details.

  • The prospectus includes all information for law college 5-year course admission Kerala.
3
Step 3

Prepare Required Documents

Gather all necessary documents before filling the application form to ensure a smooth submission.

  • 10+2 Marksheet & Certificates
  • Transfer & Conduct Certificates
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • ID Proof (Aadhar, Passport, or School ID)
  • Scholarship / Category Certificates (if applicable)
4
Step 4

Fill the Application Form

Complete the application form accurately for BBA LLB or B.Com LLB programs. Attach all required documents before submission. This is your first step for how to apply law college Kerala.

5
Step 5

Submit Application

Submit online or at the college office before the deadline. Double-check all details to avoid delays in processing.

6
Step 6

Entrance Test / Merit Evaluation

Admissions may be based on merit from qualifying exams or an entrance test, depending on the program. CFI College of Law follows fair and transparent law college admission guidelines Kerala.

7
Step 7

Interview / Counseling

Shortlisted candidates may attend counseling or an interview. Guidance on course selection, fees, and campus facilities is provided at this stage.

8
Step 8

Fee Payment & Confirmation

Pay the admission fees to secure your seat. Complete all formalities to enroll in the 5-year integrated law programs at CFI College of Law.

9
Step 9

Orientation & Commencement

Attend the orientation session to get familiar with the campus, faculty, and academic schedule. Classes begin as per the academic calendar.

Admission FAQs

Admission FAQs –
CFI College of Law

1. What courses are offered at CFI College of Law?
We offer 5-year integrated programs: BBA LLB (Hons) and B.Com LLB (Hons).
2. What is the eligibility criteria for admission?
Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent with minimum marks as per college guidelines. Students from all streams — Commerce, Science, and Arts — are eligible to apply.
3. How can I apply for admission?
You can apply online or offline by filling the application form and submitting the required documents. Follow the 9-step admission procedure outlined above for a smooth process.
4. Are internships and practical training part of the course?
Yes, students get structured internships, moot court exposure, and workshops to gain practical legal skills throughout the 5-year program.
5. Is there any support for scholarships?
Yes, we provide guidance for scholarship applications based on merit or category, as mentioned in the prospectus. Eligible students can avail government and institutional scholarships.
6. How will I know the admission status?
Shortlisted candidates are notified via email, phone, or during counseling, and further guidance is provided for fee payment and enrollment.
