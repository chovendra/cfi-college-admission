From the Desk of the Principal

A Message from
Dr. Saroja A. S.

Principal · CFI College of Law
Poyya, Thrissur, Kerala

Former Principal — All Govt. Law Colleges in Kerala
Curriculum Reformer — Kerala & MG Universities
Maritime Law Expert — Visiting Prof. at NUALS
Champion of Ethics, Integrity & Academic Discipline

My academic journey includes serving as Principal of Government Law College, Thiruvananthapuram, and holding the position of Principal across all Government Law Colleges in Kerala during my service. Throughout these years, I have worked closely with students, faculty members, universities, and regulatory authorities to uphold academic discipline, transparency, and quality standards in legal education. Maintaining an exemplary service record has always been rooted in one principle: students deserve structured guidance and sincere mentorship.

I have also had the opportunity to serve as Dean and Chairperson of the Board of Studies at Kerala University and Mahatma Gandhi University, contributing to curriculum development, academic reforms, and the strengthening of university-level legal education. As a Visiting Professor at the National University of Advanced Legal Studies (NUALS), I taught Maritime Law and engaged with advanced legal research and professional training. These experiences have shaped my understanding of how integrated law programs such as BBA LLB (Hons) and B.Com LLB (Hons) must combine theory, research, and practical exposure.

"At CFI College of Law, our focus is on building a strong academic foundation for students pursuing integrated law courses in Kerala — preparing graduates not only for court practice, but also for careers in corporate law, judiciary, public service, legal consultancy, and higher legal research."

— Dr. Saroja A. S.

Through structured classroom teaching, moot court training, internships, and research guidance, we strive to position ourselves among the emerging top law colleges in Kerala. As a BCI approved law college affiliated with the University of Calicut, we maintain strict academic standards and regulatory compliance.

Our mission is to support students seeking law admission in Kerala by providing clarity in learning, discipline in practice, and integrity in professional conduct. I warmly welcome every aspiring student to be part of an institution where legal knowledge is strengthened by ethics, dedication, and a commitment to justice.
