  • This fee is payable at the time of submitting this form.

Refund of Other Fees
  • Tuition Fee, Special Fee, Refundable Deposit, and Caution Deposit are refundable if you cancel admission in writing before the course begins.
  • Once the course commences, fees are generally non-refundable, except as permitted by University of Calicut or Bar Council of India regulations.
  • Eligible refunds will be processed within 5 working days of receiving your written cancellation request, along with original receipts.
  • Refunds are made only through the original payment method (bank transfer / DD). Cash refunds are not issued.

Note: Fees listed above cover the first year of study only. Uniform, books, stationery, examination fees, and other charges are not included and will be communicated separately.
