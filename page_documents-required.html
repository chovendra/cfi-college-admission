BBA LLB (Hons) & B.Com LLB (Hons)

Documents Required
for Admission

All submitted documents must be clear, legible, and authentic. Candidates must produce original certificates at the time of admission for verification — originals will be returned immediately after.

Mandatory Documents
01
Mandatory
Class 10th Marksheet & Certificate

Required as proof of date of birth and basic educational qualification.

02
Mandatory
Class 12th Marksheet & Certificate

Essential to verify eligibility for the 5-year integrated law programs (BBA LLB / B.Com LLB).

03
Mandatory
Transfer Certificate (TC)

Issued by the last institution attended, confirming that the student has officially left the previous institution.

04
Mandatory
Character / Conduct Certificate

A certificate from the previous institution confirming good conduct and behavior.

05
If Applicable
Migration Certificate

Required for students who have completed their qualifying examination from a board or university outside the affiliating university.

06
If Applicable
Caste Certificate (SC / ST / OBC Candidates)

Applicable for students claiming reservation benefits, issued by a competent authority.

07
If Applicable
Domicile / Nativity Certificate

Required if claiming state-based benefits or quotas.

08
Mandatory
Recent Passport-Size Photographs (4 Copies)

Photographs must be recent, clear, and with a plain background.

09
Mandatory
Aadhaar Card or Valid Government ID Proof

For identity verification and official records. Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID are accepted.

10
Mandatory
Income Certificate

Required for students applying for fee concessions, scholarships, or financial assistance schemes.

Additional Requirements (If Applicable)
A
If Applicable
Gap Year Affidavit

Mandatory if there is a break in education after completing Class 12.

B
If Applicable
Disability Certificate

Required for differently-abled candidates seeking applicable benefits.

C
If Applicable
Sports / Excellence Certificates

Necessary for students applying under sports or special achievement quotas.

Important Note
All submitted documents must be clear, legible, and authentic.
Candidates must produce original certificates at the time of admission for verification.
Original documents will be returned immediately after verification.
Incomplete documentation may delay or affect the admission process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions –
CFI College of Law

1. Why should I choose CFI College of Law?
CFI College of Law offers practical legal training, career support, modern classrooms, and a student-friendly campus in Poyya, Thrissur, Kerala.
2. What facilities are available on campus?
The campus includes smart board-equipped classrooms, a digital library, dedicated moot court, student zones, open-air theatre, and secure infrastructure.
3. Does the college provide internship support?
Yes, students receive structured internship support with guidance to gain real-world legal exposure in courts, law firms, and corporate sectors.
4. Is placement support available after completing the course?
Yes, the college provides placement support and career guidance to help students explore opportunities in advocacy, corporate law, and legal services.
5. Are the faculty members experienced?
The faculty team consists of qualified and experienced professionals who focus on practical learning, mentoring, and academic excellence.
6. Is moot court practice part of the program?
Yes, students get hands-on training through a dedicated moot court to build advocacy, drafting, and courtroom skills.
7. What documents are required for admission?
Students must submit 10th and 12th mark sheets, TC, conduct certificate, ID proof, photographs, and applicable certificates for reservation or scholarships.
8. Will original documents be returned after verification?
Yes, original certificates are verified and returned immediately after the admission process is completed.
9. Does the college support student well-being?
Yes, CFI College of Law follows anti-ragging policies and ensures a safe, supportive, and student-friendly environment.
10. How does the college prepare students for legal careers?
Through internships, workshops, mentorship, moot courts, and career guidance programs, students are prepared for professional success in the legal field.
