Management &
Academic Leadership

As a growing law college in Kerala, CFI is guided by experienced academicians and professionals committed to shaping confident and competent legal graduates. The management ensures academic standards, regulatory compliance, and student development remain top priorities.

Being a BCI approved law college affiliated with the University of Calicut, the institution maintains high standards in curriculum delivery, assessment, and student guidance.
