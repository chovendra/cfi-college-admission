A graduate of Government Law College, Ernakulam, she holds an LL.M. in Constitutional Law and Corporate Laws from the School of Legal Studies, Cochin University of Science and Technology and has successfully qualified the UGC–NET examination.

She began her academic journey in 2011 at the School of Legal Studies, Cochin University of Science and Technology after gaining practical experience in legal practice under Senior Advocate Mr. Thajudeen. This combination of practice and academia shaped her balanced approach to legal education.

In 2018, she joined the National University of Advanced Legal Studies to pursue doctoral research in Competition Law and was awarded her Ph.D. in 2026. Her research journey reflects her dedication to advancing contemporary legal scholarship.

"At CFI College of Law, she plays a vital role in nurturing a culture of disciplined academic inquiry, structured mentoring, and research-driven learning. Her focus remains on preparing students to think critically, engage responsibly, and contribute meaningfully to the evolving legal landscape."

Dr. Manasi Muralidharan's approach to legal education combines rigorous scholarship with student-centred mentoring. Her expertise across Competition Law, Corporate Laws, and Constitutional Law ensures students receive both theoretical depth and analytical clarity. She actively mentors students for moot courts, internships, and research projects — preparing them for careers in courts, corporate law firms, and public service.
