About the Program

B.Com LLB (Hons) –
5-Year Integrated
Law Program

The B.Com LLB (Hons) program at CFI College of Law is a comprehensive five-year integrated course that blends legal education with commerce and financial knowledge. Designed for students who have completed their 12th class, this program equips you with the expertise needed for careers in law, corporate finance, taxation, and business advisory.

Students gain analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and practical skills that are essential for success in legal practice, corporate law, taxation, policy-making, or entrepreneurship. With a curriculum that balances core legal knowledge and commerce subjects, the B.Com LLB (Hons) course stands out as a top choice among private law colleges in Kerala.

Through hands-on activities such as moot courts, legal research, and internships, students graduate with both academic excellence and professional confidence, ready to navigate legal and financial environments effectively.

This program is ideal for students aiming to pursue careers in law, taxation, or corporate management, under the mentorship of experienced faculty at CFI College of Law.
