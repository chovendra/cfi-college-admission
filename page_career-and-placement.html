Placement &
Internship Cell

The Placement & Internship Cell of CFI College of Law is dedicated to bridging academic excellence with professional success. As a career-focused law college in Kerala, the institution ensures that students receive structured guidance, practical exposure, and meaningful opportunities within the legal profession.

The Cell facilitates legal internships, law firm placements, court internships, and opportunities with corporate legal departments, NGOs, and senior advocates. Students are guided in securing internships with practicing advocates, district courts, High Courts, and legal service authorities to gain hands-on experience in drafting, research, and case analysis.

Regular career guidance programmes, professional skill development workshops, and interview preparation sessions are organized to enhance employability. Training includes legal drafting, communication skills, client interaction, professional ethics, and judiciary exam orientation. The Cell also supports students preparing for judicial services examinations, higher studies, and competitive exams.

"Strong industry-academia linkage and professional networking opportunities enable students to understand evolving trends in litigation, corporate law, arbitration, and compliance practice."

By combining academic learning with structured field exposure, the Cell prepares students to transition confidently into the legal profession.

Through systematic training and placement support, CFI College of Law strengthens its commitment to career readiness at this leading law college in Poyya, ensuring that graduates are professionally competent and industry-ready.
