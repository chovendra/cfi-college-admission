About Our Team

Management &
Faculty Overview

At CFI College of Law, strong academic leadership and dedicated faculty form the foundation of quality legal education. As a growing law college in Kerala, the institution is guided by experienced academicians and professionals who are committed to shaping confident and competent legal graduates.

The management ensures that academic standards, regulatory compliance, and student development remain the top priorities. With structured governance and transparent administration, the college continuously works towards strengthening its position among emerging centres for law admission in Kerala.

"Through academic discipline, personal mentoring, and professional integrity, the management and faculty collectively strive to position the institution as a trusted law college in Poyya, Thrissur, Kerala — offering meaningful legal education with long-term career value."

Our faculty members bring a combination of teaching expertise, research knowledge, and practical legal exposure. Students enrolled in BBA LLB (Hons) and B.Com LLB (Hons) programmes benefit from structured classroom learning, interactive discussions, and professional mentoring — supporting both academic excellence and career readiness.

Being a BCI approved law college affiliated with the University of Calicut, the institution maintains high standards in curriculum delivery, assessment, and student guidance. Faculty members actively encourage participation in moot courts, seminars, research activities, and internships.
