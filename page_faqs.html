Frequently Asked
Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about admissions, courses, campus life, and academic programs at CFI College of Law.

1
What are the law courses available at CFI College of Law, Poyya?
CFI Law College provides two BCI-approved integrated courses: BBA LLB (Hons.) and B.Com LLB (Hons.), suitable for 12th passed students seeking legal studies in Kerala and career-oriented law degrees.
2
Is CFI College of Law BCI approved?
Yes, CFI College of Law, Poyya is accredited by the Bar Council of India (BCI), and thus it has become one of the top law colleges in Kerala for students looking for authentic legal education.
3
Who can apply for BBA LLB at CFI Law College?
Candidates who have obtained 10+2 in any stream with a minimum of 45% marks are eligible to apply for the BBA LLB (Hons.) course at CFI Law College, which is one of the upcoming law colleges in Poyya, Kerala.
4
How does BBA LLB differ from B.Com LLB in CFI College of Law?
Although both are 5-year integrated law degrees, BBA LLB specializes in business and corporate law, while B.Com LLB specializes in commercial and finance law. Both give a solid foundation for studies in law at Poyya.
5
Can I be admitted in LLB at CFI from Bhopal or other states?
Yes, admission through LLB from Bhopal or any other state is welcome at CFI College of Law, Kerala. We invite students from all over India who want quality legal education and career preparedness.
6
Is there an entrance examination to be undergone for admission?
No, direct admission without entrance exams is offered by CFI College of Law Poyya for the right candidates, and that makes it an ideal institute for students who want to pursue law courses after 12th.
7
Why is CFI among the best law colleges in Kerala?
With actual courtroom training, BCI affiliation, and internship guidance, CFI College of Law has emerged as a leading law college in Kerala for comprehensive legal education.
8
Is CFI Law College at Poyya, Kerala?
Yes, CFI Law College has its campus in Poyya, Kerala, which provides integrated law courses in a state-of-the-art, student-centric campus with sophisticated academic and legal research infrastructure.
9
What are the career opportunities after joining CFI Law College?
Graduates can seek employment in corporate law, judiciary, litigation, legal consultancy, and public service. Several students of CFI Law College at Poyya also pursue UPSC and judicial services preparation.
10
Does CFI Law College offer placement assistance?
Yes, we provide placement guidance from the very first year, with soft skills training, internship placements, and top recruiter access for the legal sector.
11
Are internships offered in the course?
Indeed. Students in CFI College of Law, Poyya, receive 100% internship assistance with law firms, NGOs, corporate legal departments, and government ministries.
12
What infrastructure facilities are available at the campus in Poyya?
CFI provides a Wi-Fi campus, moot courts, online library, transport & hostel facilities — making it one of the most contemporary law colleges in Poyya, Kerala.
13
How long is the integrated law course at CFI Law College?
BBA LLB (Hons.) and B.Com LLB (Hons.) are both 5-year integrated courses, providing exposure to legal theory as well as practical training.
14
What makes CFI stand apart from other law colleges in Kerala?
CFI differentiates itself because of its practice-based curriculum, court simulation laboratories, personalized career mentorship, and its mission to develop the next generation of ethical, confident legal professionals.
15
Am I able to tour the campus prior to applying?
Yes, you may schedule a campus visit to see our facilities and meet with our faculty and current students at CFI Law College, Poyya.
16
What assistance does CFI offer to out-of-state students?
We provide safe hostel facilities, transport, and academic guidance, making it easier for students from Bhopal, Delhi, or other cities to adjust and excel in CFI's law programs.
17
Is legal writing and communication training part of the curriculum?
Yes, CFI includes legal writing, communication, and interview skills training as part of the curriculum to make students career-ready.
18
Does CFI organize moot court competitions?
Yes, legal aid camps and moot court sessions are a live component of CFI Law College training, giving exposure to students in the form of courtroom experiences.
19
How do I get admitted to CFI Law College?
You can request a call back or submit the enquiry form on our site online. No entrance exam is required for BBA LLB (Hons.) or B.Com LLB (Hons.) admissions.
20
Why is CFI College of Law suitable for Kerala legal studies?
Its solid academic base, emphasis on practical education, and student development, CFI College of Law, Poyya is emerging as a center for legal education in Kerala.
